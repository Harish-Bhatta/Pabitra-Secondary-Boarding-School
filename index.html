About PSBS

Pabitra Secondary Boarding School

Established in 2040 B.S., PSBS is committed to providing global standard education in a disciplined, supportive, and student-centered environment.

We nurture every learner to achieve academic excellence, confidence, and strong moral characters with modern digital integrations.

FAQ

Quick Answers

Everything you need to know at a glance.

Which classes does PSBS offer?

We teach from Play Group to Grade 10, including intensive SEE preparation with mock tests and career counseling for Grades 9–10.

When is the school office open?

The office is open Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM. You can see the live open/closed status in the Contact section.

Where is the school located?

We are at DSM – 6, Doti, Nepal. Use the “Get Directions” button in the Contact section to open the route in your maps app.

Where can I find notices and study documents?

Tap the bell icon in the top bar for the latest notices, or open Documents from the menu for PDFs, slides, and study material you can search and download.

How can I contact the school?

Call 094-411035, email info@psbs.edu.np, or send a message using the contact form at the bottom of this page.

Is there a school calendar?

Yes. Open School Calendar for events, holidays, and exam dates.
